Terms of Use

Draft for use in Canada and the United States. Last updated 13 November 2026.

These draft terms describe how we intend Agreed to be used. They are not a final contract. A lawyer still needs to review them before they are relied on or published as binding terms.

The app

Agreed is a tool for tracking subscriptions, contracts, leases and warranties, and for seeing what you may be able to save. It is offered as a prototype. Information in the app — including summaries, alerts, scripts and a Savings score — is for your convenience and may be incomplete. It is not legal, financial or tax advice, and it is not a promise that a cancellation, negotiation or claim will succeed.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the items you add and for decisions you make with merchants, banks and insurers. Please confirm dates, amounts and cancellation steps with the provider. If you connect a bank or card, you must only connect accounts you are allowed to use.

Optional bank and card connection

Connecting is optional and read-only. Login happens with a third-party provider, not in our app: your bank login goes straight to our secure provider, never to us. We cannot move money or make payments. You can switch features off or disconnect and delete that data at any time. Manual entry is always available.

Savings score and pending amounts

The Savings score is free. Amounts are counted as saved only after they are confirmed — for example, after a charge has actually stopped or dropped. Pending amounts are shown separately so the total stays honest. The score is not a currency, a cash balance, or a voucher, and it cannot be redeemed for Pro or money in this version.

Pro, notifications, and email

Pro is planned as a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It is not available to buy yet. Device reminders are optional. A private address for forwarding receipts is coming soon; until then, you may paste a receipt for a demo parse. We do not ask you to create an account to use this prototype.

Intellectual property and acceptable use

The Agreed name, design and software are owned by their rights holders. You may not misuse the app, attempt to access someone else’s accounts, or use it to break the law.

Limitation of liability

The app is provided as-is, as a prototype. To the extent allowed by the law of your province, territory or state, we are not liable for missed renewals, unsuccessful cancellations, or data stored only on your device that is lost if the device is reset. Consumer rights that cannot be waived still apply.

Changes, law, and review

These draft terms may change before they become final. They are written for people in Canada and the United States. Governing law and dispute terms should be set by a lawyer before this document is published as a contract.
