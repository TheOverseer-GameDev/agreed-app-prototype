Agreed

Privacy Policy

Draft for Canada (PIPEDA) and the United States. Last updated 17 November 2026.

Agreed (“the app,” “we”) is a prototype that helps you track subscriptions, contracts, leases, warranties, payment plans, loans, credit cards and yearly bills. This draft explains how we intend to handle information. A lawyer still needs to review it before it is relied on or published as a final policy.

Information we handle

Items you add (names, prices, dates, notes, receipts and contract text you provide) are stored on your device, in the app’s local storage. We do not operate a required account, and this prototype does not send that information to our own servers.

This includes the “Everything you pay” tools: payment plans and buy-now-pay-later plans, mortgages and loans (balance, rate, payment and renewal date), credit cards (balance, rate, due date and minimum), yearly bills, free trials, your pay schedule, and, only if you type them in, your take-home pay and the money in your account. These numbers stay on this device. They are used only for on-device estimates such as “until payday,” payoff dates and reminders. They are estimates, not financial advice.

Optional bank and card connections

Connecting a bank or card is optional. You can always add items yourself. If you connect, the login happens with a trusted, bank-grade third-party provider (the kind many apps use, such as Plaid or Flinks). We are not claiming a partnership with any named provider. Your bank login goes straight to our secure provider, never to us. We never see or store your bank password. We cannot move money or make payments. We only receive payment details — merchant name, amount, date, and whether a charge is recurring — to help find subscriptions and agreements. You can switch each related feature on or off, pause everything, or disconnect at any time. When you disconnect and delete, we delete that payment data from the app.

Canada (PIPEDA)

If you are in Canada, we intend to follow the spirit of the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA): we collect only what we need for a stated purpose, with your consent; we do not use bank-connection data for hidden purposes; we aim to keep information accurate; we use reasonable safeguards on the device; you can see what we hold in the app and ask us to correct or delete it; and you can withdraw consent by disconnecting, turning features off, or clearing the app’s data.

United States

If you are in the United States, we do not sell your personal information. We do not use a bank connection to move money. Depending on the state you live in, you may have rights to know, access, correct, or delete personal information, and to opt out of certain sharing or targeted advertising. This draft is written so those rights can be honoured; a lawyer should confirm how each state law applies before this policy is final.

Photo scan and on-device OCR

If you use photo or PDF scan, the image is read on this device using files bundled with the app. That process does not load OCR code from a content delivery network, and it does not send the image or the text to our servers.

Receipts

You can attach a receipt photo or PDF to a purchase. The file is saved in the app’s storage on this device, together with the return window, warranty and any payment plan you link. With Pro (or during the 7-day trial), “Read a receipt for me” reads the photo on this device using the same bundled text-recognition files (Tesseract). Nothing is uploaded, and the app only fills in a field when it is confident; you check every field before saving.

Document vault

The vault keeps files you choose, such as contracts, insurance policies and mortgage papers, with a title, a type, optional tags and an optional link to an item. Files are saved in the app’s storage on this device and are not uploaded. Searching by title and tag is free. With Pro (or during the trial), the app also reads the words inside a document on this device, so you can search them. That text is saved on the device with the document and is used only for search, never to fill in amounts. Deleting a document removes its file from the vault.

Your year in Agreed

The year summary is worked out on this device from what you track. Its share image is drawn on the device too. Amounts are left out of the image by default. It shows only counts unless you turn on “Include amounts in the image.” The image goes nowhere unless you tap “Share image.” That opens your phone’s share menu or, if sharing isn’t available, saves the image to your device. Once you share it, the app or person you send it to handles it under their own terms.

Household sharing and household split

Household sharing is optional and joined in person, at home, by scanning a short-lived QR code. To confirm a phone is at home, the app reads your device’s location only for household-sharing steps, such as setting your home, joining, or an at-home check, and only with your permission. It keeps only a coarse area code (a geohash covering about 75 × 150 metres), never a street address or exact coordinates, and it does not send your location to us. Only the items you choose are shared, and they pass between phones in the QR code, not through our servers. The code itself carries the key to those items, so anyone who has the code can read what you chose to share. Only show it to people in your household. Codes expire in 5 minutes, and “Make a new code” replaces the previous one. Household split divides a bill between people you name, or your household members. It can also create IOUs. The split and any IOUs stay on this device.

Pay-now links

Agreed never moves money. “Pay in your own app” copies the amount to your phone’s clipboard and can open the biller’s website or your own bank’s website (https links only). The app does not send your data to those sites; it only opens the link. Once you are there, that company’s own privacy policy applies. Other apps on your phone may be able to read the clipboard, so you may want to clear it after pasting.

Android home-screen widget

If you add the Agreed widget to your Android home screen, it shows your monthly total and your next due item: its name, the type of deadline and the date. That small summary is kept in the app’s private storage on the phone. It can be seen by anyone who can see your home screen, so remove the widget if you prefer not to show it.

Ads and partner links (planned)

On the free plan we may show a few clearly-labelled offers or ads. Partner links, if offered, are always marked Partner. If you sign up through a Partner link, the company may pay us a small fee; it does not change the price you pay, and we do not sell your information to do this. We do not add extra tracking beyond the link itself. No ad network or live partner program is connected in this prototype.

Where data lives, and how to delete it

In this prototype, your data stays on your device. You can delete bank-connection data with “Disconnect & delete data,” remove individual items in the app, use “Start fresh,” or uninstall the app and clear site data. There is no central account to close.

“Export & restore” in Settings saves a backup file (JSON) that you control. It includes your items, settings and stored files, such as receipts, vault documents and any text read from them. The backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere private and don’t email it. “Start fresh” deletes your lists, household share data, and saved files (including receipt photos and vault documents) from the app’s storage on this device. Replacing or deleting a receipt photo or a vault document also deletes the old file. You can still uninstall the app or clear its data if you want to remove the app itself.

Notifications

Reminders on this device are optional. If you turn them on, the app may schedule local alerts about renewals, trials, payment-plan and card due dates, loan renewals, yearly bills and similar deadlines. Alerts can show an item’s name on your lock screen. They respect quiet hours. You can turn them off at any time in Settings. These reminders are not used to sell you products.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your data. We don’t take a cut of what you save. We don’t use your bank login. We don’t claim certifications we haven’t earned.
