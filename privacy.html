Agreed

Privacy Policy

Draft for Canada (PIPEDA) and the United States. Last updated 13 November 2026.

Agreed (“the app,” “we”) is a prototype that helps you track subscriptions, contracts, leases and warranties. This draft explains how we intend to handle information. A lawyer still needs to review it before it is relied on or published as a final policy.

Information we handle

Items you add (names, prices, dates, notes, receipts and contract text you provide) are stored on your device, in the app’s local storage. We do not operate a required account, and this prototype does not send that information to our own servers.

Optional bank and card connections

Connecting a bank or card is optional. You can always add items yourself. If you connect, the login happens with a trusted, bank-grade third-party provider (the kind many apps use, such as Plaid or Flinks). We are not claiming a partnership with any named provider. Your bank login goes straight to our secure provider, never to us. We never see or store your bank password. We cannot move money or make payments. We only receive payment details — merchant name, amount, date, and whether a charge is recurring — to help find subscriptions and agreements. You can switch each related feature on or off, pause everything, or disconnect at any time. When you disconnect and delete, we delete that payment data from the app.

Canada (PIPEDA)

If you are in Canada, we intend to follow the spirit of the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA): we collect only what we need for a stated purpose, with your consent; we do not use bank-connection data for hidden purposes; we aim to keep information accurate; we use reasonable safeguards on the device; you can see what we hold in the app and ask us to correct or delete it; and you can withdraw consent by disconnecting, turning features off, or clearing the app’s data.

United States

If you are in the United States, we do not sell your personal information. We do not use a bank connection to move money. Depending on the state you live in, you may have rights to know, access, correct, or delete personal information, and to opt out of certain sharing or targeted advertising. This draft is written so those rights can be honoured; a lawyer should confirm how each state law applies before this policy is final.

Where data lives, and how to delete it

In this prototype, your data stays on your device. You can delete bank-connection data with “Disconnect & delete data,” remove individual items in the app, use “Start fresh,” or uninstall the app and clear site data. There is no central account to close.

Notifications

Reminders on this device are optional. If you turn them on, the app may schedule local alerts about renewals, trials and similar deadlines. You can turn them off at any time in Settings. These reminders are not used to sell you products.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell your data. We don’t take a cut of what you save. We don’t use your bank login. We don’t claim certifications we haven’t earned.

Questions about this draft can be sent once a support address is published. This page is a draft and must be reviewed by a lawyer.
